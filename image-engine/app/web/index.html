Steps — how long the model refines the picture. More = sharper, slower. 20 is a good default.
CFG — how closely it sticks to your words. 6–7 is balanced; higher can look forced.
Seed — the “dice roll”. Keep a seed to get the same image again; leave it blank for something new.
Negative — things to avoid (e.g. blurry, extra fingers, text).
