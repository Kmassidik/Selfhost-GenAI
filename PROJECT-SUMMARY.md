## ⏱️ Average render time (measured, ~24 steps)

| Output | Resolution × length | Avg render time |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny test | 512×320 × 0.2 s (5 frames) | **~1 min** |
| Short clip | 768×448 × 3 s (73 frames) | **~6 min** |
| Full-length | 512×288 × 15 s (362 frames) | **~17.5 min** |

**Rules of thumb for this box:**
- Roughly **1 minute of render per 1 second** of a 15 s clip.
- **15 s only fits at ~512×288** — that's the 8 GB VRAM ceiling (peaks ~7.5 GB).
- Trade-off is fixed: **short + sharp** _or_ **long + soft**, never both. Native 768-short-side resolution does not fit at 8 GB.

## 🔧 720p optimization (how we beat the 8 GB ceiling)

Native 720p generation of a 33B video model does not fit in 8 GB — the resident DiT activations + latent exceed VRAM (offloading only moves *weights*, not the live working set). The production-standard fix: **generate small, then AI-upscale.**

- **Generate at 1024×576** (max native that fits — ~5.7 GB VRAM after tuning).
- **Upscale** with `4x-UltraSharp.pth` (in `models/upscale_models/`) → downscale to a crisp **1280×720** (lanczos).
- **Re-mux** the model's generated 32 kHz stereo audio.

Engine flags that made 1024×576 fit (baked into the systemd service):
`--disable-smart-memory --reserve-vram 0.6` + env `PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True`.

Gotchas found by research (see knowledge-base Chapter 07):
- **SageAttention is broken for H3** — outputs pure noise (known ComfyUI bug). Keep it OFF.
- **`VAEDecodeTiled` is incompatible with H3** — H3's packed video+audio latent is a NestedTensor the tiled decoder can't handle. Use plain `VAEDecode`.
- **Raw diffusers does not help** — no sub-12 GB recipe; needs ~75 GB RAM. ComfyUI's pruned int8 build is the leanest path.
- **Turbo-LoRA** (`larryvrh/MiniMax-H3-Turbo-Lora`) cuts sampling ~20 → 4–8 steps (~5× faster) — not yet wired in, biggest available speed win.

ComfyUI now runs as a **systemd service** (`comfyui.service`) — auto-starts on boot, auto-restarts on failure.

## ✅ What we did

1. **Bare-metal setup** — installed the NVIDIA driver, verified the GPU, built an isolated Python 3.12 environment (system Python 3.14 was too new for PyTorch), installed PyTorch (CUDA), ComfyUI, and the required node packs.
2. **Got the model running** — downloaded ~46 GB of H3 weights (33B DiT + Qwen3-VL-32B encoder + video & audio VAEs), and solved the traps along the way:
   - `/tmp` was a 31 GB RAM-disk → staged downloads overflowed it → moved to real disk.
   - The first DiT was a GGUF built for stable-diffusion.cpp → ComfyUI rejected it → switched to the official **native int8 safetensors**.
   - HuggingFace throttled the big download → fixed with `curl -L --http1.1 -C - --retry` and an HF token.
3. **Generated 6 clips** with synchronized audio (table above), up to a true **720p**.
4. **Learned the craft** — discovered H3's **official structured prompt format** (the biggest quality lever) and built a reusable template.
5. **Optimized for 720p** — parallel research + a generate-at-1024×576-then-upscale pipeline (details above).
6. **Hardened the engine** — ComfyUI as a persistent systemd service with low-VRAM tuning.
7. **Built a knowledge base** — an 8-page offline learning site in `knowledge-base/` with Mermaid diagrams and all the real specs.

## ✍️ The prompt recipe (biggest quality lever)

H3 wants a **structured** prompt, not plain sentences. Fill this template and swap the details:

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Cinematic, <framing>, <subject + scene>.
    The camera <push in / pan / tracking> with small amplitude at slow speed as <action>.
    The <voice description> (S1) says: <d>[English] your line here.</d> <more action>.

overall_soundscape: <ambient + action sounds, 1–4 sentences>.

non_diegetic_music: <instruments, tempo, dynamics — or N/A>.
```

Tips: keep it **one shot** for coherence · write camera motion as natural verbs · wrap spoken lines in `<d>[English]…</d>` · never rely on readable on-screen text (no diffusion video model can spell).

Full official guide: `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/docs/VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_base_en.md`
